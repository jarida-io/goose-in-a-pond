Architecture

Goose In A Pond, top to bottom

Three layers, all on one board in the house: the surfaces people use, Goose and its MCP tools in the middle, and a local inference engine underneath. Phones reach the pond over the home network, or a private tailnet (tsnet + Headscale) when away.
