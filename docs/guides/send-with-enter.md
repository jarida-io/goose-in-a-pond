# Sending a chat message with Enter

By default, pressing **Enter** in a chat box sends the message, and **Shift + Enter** starts a new
line. This holds in the hub's chat, the classic chat and the home screen's "Ask me, or tell me what
to run" bar.

## Turning it off

1. Open the menu (☰, top left).
2. Choose **Settings**.
3. At the bottom of the left-hand list, under **This app**, choose **Appearance**.
4. Under **Typing**, switch off **Enter sends the message**.

With it off, **Enter** starts a new line and **Ctrl + Enter** (**Cmd + Enter** on a Mac) sends.

The choice belongs to the screen it was made on, like the theme: the pond's panel, a laptop and a
phone can each have their own. It is kept in that browser's storage, so clearing the browser's
site data turns it back on.
